MergeKitchen

Merge ingredients, build burgers and feed customers before they walk away.

How to play

1Tap a product icon by the shelves below (bun, patty, veggies, cheese) to send a raw ingredient to the table. Each product has a cooldown you can skip with 💎.
2Tap a tile on the table, then tap another tile of the same level — they merge into the next level (e.g. dough + dough → bun).
3Max out the bun, patty, veggies and cheese, then tap the finished ingredient → tap the dark "Build Burger" panel next to the table to add it. Collect all 4 to get a finished burger 🍔.
4Fries, desserts and drinks don't go on the burger panel — just max them out right on the table and they're ready to sell.
5Tap a finished dish on the table → tap a matching order ticket on the right to fulfil it and earn coins 🪙 and XP.
6Every order has patience: the customer's face gets grumpier over time (😊→😐→😠→😡), then the order disappears with no penalty. The +💎 button on a ticket resets the timer if you're running out of time.
7XP levels you up and unlocks new products (fries, desserts, drinks, and nuggets at level 7) plus kitchen decor. Decor gets a gold tier at level 9, and level 10 — the max — earns you the "master chef" title. The banner at the top always shows what to do next.
